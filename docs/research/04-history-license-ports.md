- Wayback snapshots were retrieved with `curl` from `web.archive.org/web/<timestamp>id_/...`; the timestamp in the URL is the capture date, i.e. "text existed by then", not "text was written then".

## 2. Author & end of development

**Identity (as publicly stated by himself):**
- "TheyCallMeDanger (Richard H. Clark)", co-copyright "Megan Lorraine Clark" ([P] legal 2014). Steam page: "Richard H. Clark / TheyCallMeDanger" ([P] Steam). GoFundMe organizer "Richard Clark, Olney, MD" ([P] GoFundMe mirror).
- About page: "started this whole adventure for his son … programming for over 30 years"; tools Eclipse, Techne (models), GIMP. MeganLorraine did website and in-game art in Photoshop/Illustrator CS6 ([P] [about mirror](https://shrekleaker.github.io/orespawn.com/about.html)).
- PMC 2014: "To AnimalTipper (my son) for tons of great ideas!!!!"; voice credit "Jess" ([P] PMC snapshot 2016).
- Credited model contributors: Copperfist, Dark Leviathan, Viorp, Pampanapa, Darkstar108, BlueEyes9, Heltrato, LordBowin, Kitsu, CakeBake, Tyranno66, Alexwii3, MrRockstar1539, epicdjs, UncleOil, The GnomeWorks, MTX1996, KarryBird, Pixel; code credit Nico Bergemann (input packets) ([P] [credits mirror](https://shrekleaker.github.io/orespawn.com/credits.html)). PMC 2014: "while not all of the critter models are my own, all the animation and entities that go with them are 100%."
- A 2021 PMC re-post names an "Official OreSpawn Team": Conquerent321, TheyCallMeRandom, Reaper, AnimalTipper, Megan Lorraine ([P?] [PMC page, reposted 2021](https://www.planetminecraft.com/mod/orespawn-mod-2090519/) — uploaded by a third party, "altough I have retired").

**Why development on Minecraft stopped (three phases):**
1. **Stop at 1.7.10 (2014/15).** No dated primary statement found. Claims:
   - Lag and Forge/MC changes (author relayed on a 2016 forum thread) [S].
   - Not wanting to work for free for Microsoft. The community doc quotes a YouTube comment by the author: "The point is, that it is MINE and I gain absolutely NOTHING from updating it to the latest MC/MS. Not gonna happen." [S][?]
   - The author's own site framed it as "OreSpawn has outgrown MC … DangerZone has no EULA!" [P].
2. **Paid/crowdfunded 1.12.2 comeback (2020–21)** → cancelled amid the Discord and leak conflict [S].
3. **Final farewell (2024)** — verbatim, [P] homepage capture 2024-10-04 / 2025-01-01:
   > "It's time to say goodbye! Yeah, it's been a good run, but OreSPawn and Dangerzone are antiques now. Hey guys! Yes, I've decided it's time to move on. OreSpawn and DangerZone are old and moldy antiques by now. It is time to let them rest in peace. So grab a copy of the DangerZone source code while you can! (downloads page) This website won't last much longer. I will be taking it down. As for me, I'm off to a new life adventure. Moving to where it's warm. I need to be near the ocean, the sunlight, the sand, and the saltwater. Thank you all for the support and an experience I will never forget."

**Sale/transfer rumor:** **no evidence found** of a sale or rights transfer. The community doc mentions a video in which the author refuses "to sell out". The 2026 domain parking and reconstruction concern the domain, not the IP. Unverified [?].

**Cultural impact (sourced claims):**
- "one of the oldest Minecraft mods", "highly polarizing", "highly unbalanced … often left out of modpacks" ([S] [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/minecraft-orespawn-mod-features-installation-guide)).
- PMC capture 2016: 2,745,219 views, 925,625 downloads, 362 favorites on the 1.7.10 page ([P]).
- The creator controversy is now a YouTube genre in its own right (CalebIsSalty, 335k views; Straunge).

**Other facts:**
- **Source code of the Minecraft mod was never released** as far as found. A 2016 forum post says it is "closed source" and "copyrighted" [S]; all GitHub copies are decompilations (§5).
- **DangerZone source *was* released** as build-kits (DZ 2.2, DZVR 2.7, DZVR 2.8 multiplayer; MediaFire links on the download page) [P]. The DangerZone legal text restricts it:
  - "Absolutely no permission is given to use anything (code, textures, models, sounds, etc) from the DangerZone game outside of the DangerZone game itself. In other words, no porting of the DangerZone game, in whole or in part, to other games."
  - "Absolutely no permission is given to use anything from the OreSpawn for DangerZone mod outside of the DangerZone game."
  - "All of the OreSpawn for DangerZone mod is Copyright TheyCallMeDanger/Richard H. Clark."
  - Only labeled parts of the DZ 1.9.3 buildkit became public domain on 2021-01-01; "DZ 2.0 and beyond are NOT public domain." ([P-mirror] DangerZone Archive legal)
  - The farewell's "grab a copy of the DangerZone source code" is **not** a license grant for porting into Minecraft.
- **Enforcement history (no formal DMCA record found):**
  - InsanityCraft (CurseForge re-upload 2024): "we had to delete the old Curseforge page because TheyCallMeDanger threatened to sue us and then latter on Curseforge removed all OreSpawn remakes, now we are back because we realize it is just empty threats" [P — port author's claim, the CurseForge side is unverified] ([CF InsanityCraft](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/insanitycraft)).
  - jtrent238/OreSpawnDzPort README: "This will not be continued because the Owner of Orespawn (TheyCallMeDanger) does not want anyone to attempt to update/port Orespawn in anyway." [P] ([GitHub](https://github.com/jtrent238/OreSpawnDzPort))
  - Community doc: InsanityCraft dev BikerBones — "He basically pitched a fit about the artwork, saying his daughter made it, and we're stealing from him"; organized mass reporting of repost sites (9minecraft, Aternos) [S].
  - Lumen/DMCA database **not checked** [?].
- **Current successors mostly avoid original assets** and state it explicitly: Chaos Awakens ("Every single asset is made by this development team!", "not a gray hat backdoor to 'porting'"), Antarchy ("uses no assets or code from the original"), SmoreSpawn ("100% original"). Some do not:
  - OSCE: "I dont copied the code from the original mod I just picked the textures and models".
  - Legacy/Echoes of Utopia: "archival assets from the 1.7.10 mod", "Transformative Preservation Project".
  - AdventureKraft: used "textures from the OreSpawn mod for DangerZone the game" and "got permission from a few of the original model creator" (sources in §4).

### 3.2 General considerations (not legal advice; jurisdiction-dependent)
- **Copyright is independent of availability.** An abandoned, unlisted or "taken down" mod stays under copyright (for an individual author in the US and EU: generally life + 70 years). "Abandonware" or "preservation" is not a license. The author's last documented position (Phase D) is an explicit refusal.
- **Rights are fragmented.** Per the author, about a quarter of the content (mostly creature models) belongs to third-party modelers who granted rights only to OreSpawn. Even an author grant would not cover those, and vice versa. The 2014 "submitted models → you give up rights" clause is a unilateral website statement whose effect on those contributors is unclear [?].
- **Lowest-risk path: clean-room re-implementation.** That means new code, **own** textures, models, sounds and animations, no decompiled code as reference for copying, and no "OreSpawn" in the project name. Only the Phase B text ever permitted redistribution or modification with credit; it was withdrawn, and it never addressed ports.
- **Game mechanics and ideas** (ant-portal dimensions, a giant boss, an over-the-top sword) are generally not protected by copyright. Concrete expression is: textures, models, sound files, code, distinctive text. Successors such as Chaos Awakens and Antarchy follow exactly this line.
- **Names:**
  - "OreSpawn": no trademark search was done (USPTO/EUIPO) [?]. Using it in a project title invites confusion and takedown requests regardless.
  - Some OreSpawn names evoke **third-party** marks ("Mobzilla" → Godzilla/Toho; "Ghidorah" appears in AdventureKraft). A remake should rename these independently of the OreSpawn question.
- **Decompiled repos** (history-craft, mglucas0123) put someone else's code under GPL/no license. That grants **no** rights; do not base a remake on them.
- **Monetization:** Minecraft's EULA/usage guidelines restrict paywalled mod content (the 2020 "$5 to play" plan drew exactly that criticism) ([Minecraft EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula)).

### 3.3 Open license questions
- Did any of the credited modelers publicly license their OreSpawn models? AdventureKraft claims permissions via an Imgur gallery ([imgur LEQcrvR](https://imgur.com/gallery/LEQcrvR), could not be fetched).
- Is "OreSpawn" a registered trademark? Not checked.
- Was a formal DMCA notice filed with CurseForge? Unverified; only the port author's claim exists.
- Is the author still reachable for a permission request? The site is gone; a contact address was published on the 2020 homepage. Given Phase D, a grant is unlikely.

### 4.2 Notes per project
- **Chaos Awakens.** Founded by "the staff of the DangerZone Modding Discord" after the 1.12.2 cancellation ([site](https://chaosawakens.github.io/)). It says it is not affiliated with OreSpawn or its creator. Several of its members reportedly worked on the original ("ARandomUser/MrRandomUser … developers on the original mod … now working on its successor, Chaos Awakens" — [S] Sportskeeda).
  - Mixed license: code up to Beta 0.8d is LGPL-2.1, later code and all assets ARR ([LICENSE.MD](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChaosAwakens/ChaosAwakens/1.16.5-forge/LICENSE.MD)). Old LGPL code is technically reusable under LGPL terms, but it is 1.16.5 Forge and GeckoLib 3.
  - Public releases only for 1.16.5 (Modrinth, published 2022-10-23). Branches `1.20.1-multiloader` and `-staging` exist; per a search snippet, after 0.12.0.0 "the team moved to Minecraft 1.20.1" [S].
- **Antarchy.**
  - Most current and technically closest to the user's target (1.21.1 NeoForge). Requires Integrated API, GeckoLib, Sizeable Foliage; Infinite Dimensions optional ([CF](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/antarchy), [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/antarchy)).
  - Explicitly excludes from OreSpawn: girlfriends, criminals, coins, emerald gear, bees, dried spawn eggs, T-shirts, mosquitoes (Modrinth FAQ).
  - A remake on the same platform would compete directly with it.
- **Craziness Awakened.** Dependencies GeckoLib, Pehkui, AttributeFix ([Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/craziness-awakened-(orespawn-remake))). Pehkui points to entity scaling and AttributeFix to lifting the vanilla attribute caps — both relevant for giant bosses (inference).
- **OSCE.** Built with MCreator, 16 releases in three weeks (July 2025), then stopped. It uses extracted original textures and models. Its line "Any OreSpawn remake can use the assets that I created" does **not** cover those.
- **Legacy/Echoes of Utopia.** Explicit disclaimer "I claim no ownership over the original OreSpawn property (owned by Richard Clark / TheyCallMeDanger)" while shipping the original assets ([Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/legacy-of-utopia)). This illustrates the risk class a remake should avoid.
- **AdventureKraft.** The lead (Katara/DevMaster1015) was the central opponent in the 2020/21 Discord conflict [S]. The Team Olympus page says: "This is not a port of OreSpawn it's a remake"; "The mod is in 1.16.1 1.16.4, and 1.16.5" ([Team Olympus](https://officialteamolympus.weebly.com/adventurekraft.html)).

## 6. Why ports stalled

1. **The author's opposition and legal threats.**
   - InsanityCraft pulled its CurseForge page after a threat to sue.
   - jtrent238 abandoned his port "because the Owner … does not want anyone to attempt to update/port".
   - The author's organized mass reporting against repost sites (community doc) [P/S].
   - Result: projects either stopped or rebranded as "spiritual successors" with all-new assets.
2. **Scope.**
   - "Over One Hundred NEW MOBS!" plus 5+ dimensions and hundreds of items (PMC 2014) [P].
   - The author himself asked for help on the 1.12.2 port: "OreSpawn/MC NEEDS MODDERS!!! Yes, it's too big!" [P homepage 2020-09].
   - Early fan ports "only [had] a few basic blocks, items, and gear sets" [S community doc].
   - AdventureKraft's CurseForge page is still an item list after five years [P].
3. **Performance.**
   - The author in 2016: "the mod already causes epic amounts of lag" [S].
   - The mglucas0123 repo exists solely to fix lag in OreSpawn dimensions [P].
   - Giant entities (Mobzilla, Kraken, the King) and dense dimensions are the likely culprits (inference). Modern remakes add scaling and attribute libraries (Pehkui, AttributeFix, GeckoLib).
4. **Engine churn.**
   - The author named Forge/MC changes; the community doc says custom dimensions after 1.12 are "allegedly hard" compared to 1.6.4 [S].
   - Chaos Awakens has shipped only 1.16.5 since 2022 while rewriting for 1.20.1 multiloader [P GitHub branches].
5. **Asset creation cost.** Clean successors (Chaos Awakens, Antarchy) create every model, texture and animation themselves, the main throughput limit. The shortcut (OSCE, Utopia mods) reuses originals and carries the legal risk.
6. **Community fragmentation.** Discord terminations, bans of port supporters, and competing "official" servers in 2020/21 split the modding community [S].
7. **Tooling shortcuts don't scale.** MCreator-based OSCE shipped 16 versions in three weeks and then stopped (inference from the file dates).

## 7. Community priorities (what people remember)

Evidence-based signals, strongest first:
- **Signature bosses and mascots.**
  - The author's own tagline: "Girlfriends, Kraken, Mobzilla, Zoo Cages, Ray Guns, tons of new ores, new plants, and 5 new Dimensions" [P homepage 2014].
  - The community doc names "The Royals" (King, Queen, Prince, Princess) plus Mobzilla, Big Bertha, the Kraken, Girlfriends and the Ultimate Set as "the most recognizable faces of the mod" [S].
  - Two separate 2026 mods exist solely for the King and Queen, plus *OreSpawn Boss*.
- **Miner's Dream.** Four independent single-item mods on Modrinth (2023–2026) recreate just this tunnel-digging item. That is a strong "small feature, big nostalgia" signal.
- **Ant/termite portals to dimensions.** Kept by Chaos Awakens, Antarchy (Brown/Red/Termite/Rainbow ants) and OSCE (Red Ant, Danger Ant, Termite).
- **Absurdly overpowered gear:** Big Bertha, Royal Guardian set, Ultimate sword, armor and bow, the Slice (OSCE, Antarchy "Ultimate Gear"). Sportskeeda notes crafts "that work closer to cheats". Chaos Awakens reportedly keeps the mod "way too overpowered to work with other mods" [S snippet, Chaos Awakens wiki].
- **Rideables and toys:** hoverboards (Orespawn Revived), dragon riding, critter/zoo cages, Apple Cows (standalone mod exists), Popcorn, Magic Apples (PMC feature list).
- **What successors deliberately drop:** Girlfriends/Boyfriends (Craziness Awakened, Antarchy), criminals, coins/ads, emerald gear, bees, dried spawn eggs, T-shirts, mosquitoes (Antarchy FAQ). Girlfriends are iconic but divisive; OSCE is the only one that leans into them.
- **Balance criticism is part of the identity:** "highly unbalanced … often left out of modpacks" [S Sportskeeda]. Modern successors add configs; Antarchy advertises "a ridiculous amount of config options" [S search snippet].
- **Reddit nostalgia threads could not be read** (reddit.com, old.reddit and fandom were blocked for fetching) → gap.
